# Templates and adoption strategy

Teptop must feel like a framework people can adopt quickly for real work. That means a broad template catalog, a strong plugin story, and clear examples across many industries.

## Template catalog

The repo now expands the product story with templates for:

- SaaS dashboard and product marketing
- Portfolio and personal brand site
- E-commerce storefront and catalog
- Internal tool and admin console
- App-lite MVP or small business product

## Adoption and market fit

To compete with the market, a framework needs to feel useful to both product teams and startups:

- Faster shipping for small teams
- Clean architecture for internal tools and SaaS
- SEO-friendly marketing pages
- Cost-effective deployment
- Good DX for solo builders and full teams

## Ecosystem levers

- Plugin marketplace and examples
- Template library for common product types
- Deploy-to-cloud presets
- Community starter repos
- Benchmark and release quality metrics

## Job market and hiring angle

Teams are increasingly looking for frameworks that are:

- quick to learn,
- easy to ship with,
- simple to monitor in production,
- adaptable to different business domains,
- and not locked into a single use case.

Teptop can win this narrative by emphasizing speed, simplicity, and product ergonomics instead of just low-level runtime internals.
